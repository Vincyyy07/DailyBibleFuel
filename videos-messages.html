Video Transcript & Sermon Study Outline

[00:00 - 05:15] Introduction: The Geography of the Soul

"Grace and peace to you in Jesus' mighty name. When David writes Psalm 23, he does not speak as a cloistered monk who was insulated from danger. He speaks as a shepherd and an outlaw who knew the rugged ravine of Judea..."

[05:16 - 12:40] The Hebrew Context: Gai Tzalmaveth (The Valley of Deep Darkness)

"The Hebrew phrasing here is not a poetic embellishment. It depicts a crevasse where midday sun never penetrates. Yet notice the shift in pronouns: David stops talking *about* God ('He leads me', 'He restores my soul') and begins talking *to* God ('for You are with me')..."

[12:41 - 24:15] Conclusion & Prayer of Impartation

"Your rod and staff comfort me. Today, whatever trial whispers defeat, confess the everlasting covenant of Christ over your family. Let us bow our heads in prayer..."
